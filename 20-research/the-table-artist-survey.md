# The Table — Artist Participant Survey

**Client:** Haley Dall / The Table  
**Purpose:** Capture artist participant stories and community impact to power donor outreach content and demonstrate tangible ROI  
**Delivery:** Mobile-optimized, text-distributable via WhatsApp/iMessage group threads  
**Format:** Upsight rich-media survey (video + text responses supported)

---

## Desired End-State (Working Backwards)

Before writing questions, here is what we want donors to *feel and see* after the survey content is compiled:

1. **Emotional connection** — A donor watches a 30-second clip of an artist describing their before/after journey and thinks: *"I want to be part of that story."*
2. **Tangible ROI** — A donor understands that supporting The Table = artists re-engaging local businesses, venues, and neighborhoods = measurable local economic impact.
3. **Urgency** — A donor understands what would be *lost* if The Table disappeared, creating a compelling giving narrative.
4. **Sound bites ready for use** — Clips suitable for donor videos, social reels, website profiles, and grant applications.

---

## Survey Design Notes

- **Keep it to ~6 core questions** (Q1–Q6) so completion rates stay high over text/phone
- **Q3 ("What would be lost")** is the highest-value donor sound bite question — prioritize for video prompt
- **Q1 ("What is it like to be an artist right now")** surfaces both discouraged and hopeful voices — both are usable for donor storytelling
- **Anonymous option (Q0a)** should remain available but not default — most participants in a tight-knit cohort will be comfortable sharing
- **Mobile-first:** Confirm survey renders cleanly on iPhone (Apple group text distribution) and WhatsApp
- **CRM linking:** Use phone number tracking in Upsight to tie responses back to participant profiles
- **Urgency framing for distribution message:**  
  > *"Hey [Name], we're putting together something special to share with our supporters. If you have 5 minutes, we'd love to hear your story — and if you're up for it, even a quick video. Hear back by [Tuesday/Wednesday]. Thanks for being part of this."*
