# Startup San Diego x Innovate78 Phase 2

## SOW Cost Guidance for UpSight Services + Platform

Prepared as internal pricing guidance for shaping a Phase 2 statement of work. This should not be inserted into the presentation deck as-is.

## Executive Recommendation

Position UpSight as the specialist intelligence operations + software partner underneath Startup San Diego's larger Innovate78 contract.

Assumption: Startup San Diego may receive roughly **$150,000** for the overall Phase 2 program. UpSight should not consume the whole budget. Startup San Diego needs room for its own staff time, local relationship work, program management, reporting, and profit.

Recommended economics:

| Budget Layer | Target |
|---|---:|
| Likely StartupSD prime contract | ~$150,000 |
| StartupSD retained budget / margin / staff delivery | $80,000-$95,000 |
| UpSight services + platform target | $55,000-$70,000 |
| Pass-through enrichment/tools | $3,000-$5,000 |

Recommended first offer:

| Component | Recommended Price |
|---|---:|
| UpSight partner services fixed fee | $42,000 |
| UpSight platform license, first 12 months | $18,000 |
| Data enrichment pass-through budget | $4,000 |
| **Total UpSight Year 1 SOW** | **$64,000** |

Negotiation range:

| Tier | Best For | Total Services | Platform | Estimated Total |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Lean | UpSight sets up system and coaches StartupSD internal team | $26,000-$32,000 | $15,000-$18,000 | $41,000-$50,000 |
| Recommended | Strong partner role while preserving StartupSD economics | $38,000-$48,000 | $18,000-$24,000 | $56,000-$72,000 |
| Stretch | Deeper UpSight execution support, still partner-led | $58,000-$68,000 | $24,000 | $82,000-$92,000 |

The cleanest number to put in an SOW is **$60k-$68k all-in for UpSight Year 1**, with enrichment pass-through capped and pre-approved. If StartupSD is trying to preserve more profit, offer a **$45k-$50k partner pilot** with tighter scope.

Important pricing note: this is **not priced as an outsourced research build**. It is a partner enablement package. Startup San Diego contributes internal people for source collection, local relationship management, outreach coordination, meeting follow-up, and much of the manual verification. UpSight provides the higher-leverage layer: architecture, software, data process design, QA model, analysis patterns, enablement, and selective specialist execution.

## Pricing Logic

This is not just research, but it also should not be sold as UpSight taking over all execution. The offer combines:

- Data operations consulting
- Ecosystem research enablement
- Primary outreach design
- CRM/data architecture
- Data enrichment workflow and QA standards
- Portal/workspace setup
- Ongoing intelligence operations training
- Software license for UpSight as the system of record and evidence layer

Comparable 2026 rates support the value of the specialist work, but avoid putting hourly math in the client-facing SOW. The better frame is a fixed-fee partner package: UpSight brings the operating system, templates, QA standards, software, and senior guidance; Startup San Diego brings internal capacity and local relationships.

Sources reviewed for market anchoring:

- VantaInsights 2026 market research cost guide cites BLS May 2025 market research analyst wage data as a labor baseline before benefits, overhead, and consulting markup.
- Data Consulting Firms 2026 data engineering benchmark places senior US onshore data engineers around the mid-$100s/hr.
- GSA schedule examples show 2026-2027 net hourly rates for business analyst, software engineer, agile coach, analyst, and data scientist roles ranging from low $100s/hr into the high $200s/hr and beyond.
- ConsultNonprofit's 2026 nonprofit consulting guide places many US nonprofit consultants in a $125-$350/hr range, with senior strategy above that.

## Recommended SOW Structure

Use deliverable-based scope, not hour-based scope. The SOW should make clear that Startup San Diego is the local execution lead and UpSight is the intelligence operations partner.

### Phase 2A: Intelligence Operations Foundation

Purpose: make Startup San Diego the orchestrator of a repeatable ecosystem intelligence process, not just a list-builder.

UpSight role: lead the architecture and operating model. Startup San Diego owns source gathering, partner coordination, and internal program management.

Deliverables:

- Intake and audit of existing Startup San Diego / Innovate78 data sources
- Data inventory framework covering Mailchimp, StartupSD lists, prior event lists, partner city records, campus sources, public websites, and known super-connectors
- Gap analysis against best-practice ecosystem intelligence operations
- Data model for organizations, people, relationships, sectors, city, stage, needs, programs, and evidence sources
- Source-of-truth design for how new information enters the system
- Research operations playbook for repeatable collection, QA, enrichment, and reporting by StartupSD staff

Recommended partner fee: **$10,000-$12,000**

### Phase 2B: Ecosystem Research + Data Wrangling

Purpose: identify who is building in each Innovate78 city and create a clean, queryable relationship database.

UpSight role: provide the data model, dedupe/enrichment workflow, QA standards, and selected wrangling support. Startup San Diego owns most source collection and local verification.

Deliverables:

- Consolidation framework for organization and founder data across Carlsbad, Escondido, Oceanside, San Marcos, Vista, CSUSM, and MiraCosta
- Dedupe and normalization workflow for existing sources
- Classification by city, sector, stage, cluster, business type, and support need
- Contact enrichment workflow for priority organizations and people
- Evidence notes showing where records came from and how confidence was assigned
- Super-connector and ecosystem actor tagging schema

Recommended partner fee: **$12,000-$16,000**

Pass-through enrichment budget:

- Lean: $3,000-$5,000
- Recommended: $4,000-$5,000
- Expanded: $7,500-$10,000

Assumption: pass-through tools may include contact databases, email validation, domain enrichment, and light manual verification. Keep this capped and separately approved.

### Phase 2C: Primary Research + Feedback Loops

Purpose: add live intelligence from the people closest to the ecosystem, rather than relying only on web research.

UpSight role: design the outreach/survey/interview system, provide templates, and synthesize signals. Startup San Diego conducts or co-conducts local conversations.

Deliverables:

- Survey/outreach design for super-connectors, founders, local businesses, and partner city stakeholders
- Interview guide or conversational prompts
- Super-connector conversations supported by UpSight templates and synthesis; Startup San Diego leads scheduling and local relationship handling
- Lightweight synthesis framework for needs, gaps, program fit, and referrals
- Feedback loop design so new conversations continue improving the database

Recommended partner fee: **$6,000-$8,000**

### Phase 2D: Portal, Reporting + Enablement

Purpose: turn the data into something Innovate78 can use to right-size economic development efforts.

UpSight role: configure the workspace, views, query patterns, and reporting model; Startup San Diego remains the client-facing program lead.

Deliverables:

- UpSight workspace configured for Innovate78 / Startup San Diego workflows
- Table and map views for ecosystem exploration
- Queryable filters by city, sector, stage, cluster, program fit, needs, and relationship strength
- CRM-style engagement tracking for ongoing lead generation and follow-up
- Reporting views for city partners and leadership
- Training session(s) so Startup San Diego can operate as research architects
- Final readout support with recommendations for where Innovate78 can maximize economic growth

Recommended partner fee: **$10,000-$12,000**

## SOW Assumptions

Use these assumptions to avoid scope creep:

- Partner cities: Carlsbad, Escondido, Oceanside, San Marcos, Vista
- Campus sources: CSUSM and MiraCosta
- Phase 1 super-connector list is already underway and treated as input, not a repeated deliverable
- UpSight is responsible for orchestration, data architecture, tooling, enrichment workflow, analysis, and enablement
- Startup San Diego remains the program owner and local relationship lead
- Innovate78 / city partners provide available source lists and stakeholder introductions
- Contact enrichment costs are pass-through or capped
- Public-facing directory scope should be explicitly defined before launch
- Legal/privacy review for publishing names, emails, or company details is client responsibility unless added to scope

## Payment Structure

Recommended:

- 40% on signature
- 30% after data model + initial consolidated dataset
- 20% after portal/reporting views
- 10% after final training/readout

Alternative:

- Services billed fixed-fee by milestone
- Platform license billed annually upfront
- Enrichment billed as pre-approved pass-through

## What to Emphasize in Positioning

The strongest value statement is not “we will build a database.”

Use:

> This gives Innovate78 a living intelligence system for maximizing economic growth: identifying businesses that need support, surfacing companies that can help others, matching city programs to actual demand, and giving Startup San Diego a repeatable data practice it can operate and scale.

For Startup San Diego:

> This positions Startup San Diego as a credible data and services partner to Innovate78 and future EDCs, chambers, municipalities, and regional coalitions.

For Innovate78:

> This helps Innovate78 right-size economic development efforts around real ecosystem signals, not anecdotes or static lists.

## Open Questions Before Final Quote

- How many source lists are available, and in what shape?
- Do they expect all existing businesses or primarily startups/innovation-oriented companies?
- How many records should be enriched in Phase 2?
- Are emails/contacts intended for internal CRM use only or public directory display?
- How many stakeholder/super-connector conversations are expected?
- Is the portal public-facing, partner-only, or both?
- Who owns follow-up outreach after the first dataset is built?
- Should the license be sold to Startup San Diego, Innovate78, or jointly?
