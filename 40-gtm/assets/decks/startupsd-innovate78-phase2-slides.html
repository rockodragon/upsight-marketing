Phase 2 Deliverables

Program and platform

1. Data audit and gap analysis

You receive: a written audit of every current list — fields, sources, consent status, duplicates, coverage — and a named list of what is missing.

2. The ecosystem dataset

You receive: one classified dataset across Carlsbad, Escondido, Oceanside, San Marcos, Vista, CSUSM and MiraCosta, deduplicated, with a source and confidence on every record.

3. Run the collection

You receive: completed surveys and interviews at a stated coverage rate, including follow-up on non-responders. This is the part that does not happen on its own, and it is where most of the effort goes.

4. The live portal

You receive: access to a queryable workspace — table, map, filters, AI query — plus CRM-style follow-up tracking. Hosted and maintained, not handed over as files.

5. The briefing and the filing export

You receive: a quarterly briefing answering the questions the cities actually asked, and a static export formatted for whatever report Innovate78 and the cities owe their funders.

Items 1, 2, 3 and 5 are deliverables — things handed to you on a date. Item 4 is access to a capability, which is why it is priced as a license rather than a milestone.

Data, Consent and Publication

What can be shared, and what cannot

Three rules govern the system.

Public facts are publishable

  • Company name, city, sector, size, public web presence.
  • No consent required. This is the backbone of the directory.

Anything a person said needs consent

  • Quotes, stated needs, survey answers, interview material.
  • Consent is recorded per person and covers a named use — internal analysis, partner reporting, or public attribution. They are not the same permission.

Small numbers get suppressed

  • "Three companies in Vista said X" can identify all three.
  • Findings below the agreed threshold are reported as suppressed, in words, rather than shown.

Raw interview material stays in the UpSight workspace; partners receive findings and exports. Worth settling early, and straightforward to navigate.
