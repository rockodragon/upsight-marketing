# Build prompt — The Thing You Already Know

**Slug:** `outreach-asks`
**Composition:** `TheThingYouAlreadyKnow` (in `05-remotion`)
**Cuts:** master 3:14 · `:30` paid · `:15` social

## Job

The prospect recognizes their own situation in the first 20 seconds, and by
the end wants the loop running on their own customers.

**The one idea:** *You already had the answer. It was in a conversation nobody could search.*

## Creative brief

- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 194s master
- **Use:** homepage hero, paid traffic, first sales touch
- **Tone:** confident, quiet, a little haunting at the open. No hype, no
  stock-photo optimism, no "AI-powered." The product is the proof, not the promise.
- **CTA:** Upload a conversation — not book a demo.
- **Pace:** slow open (they need to feel the scar), fast middle (the loop
  should feel effortless), slow close (let the idea land).
- **Silence is a tool.** The two beats after "In August, they left" are the
  most persuasive seconds. Don't score over them.
- **No logo in Act I.** The moment a brand appears, recognition becomes advertising.
- **No feature names on screen.** Quotes, people, groups, a question going back out.
- **Every number on screen must be treated as real.** 14 people means 14 people.
  v1 uses an illustrative cast of 14 named customers; swap for a live project later.

## Scene structure

1. **Act I (0:00–0:28)** — Black. White text. Waveform. Transcript. No logo.
2. **Act II (0:28–0:55)** — Artifact wall → grey folders. Villain is non-accumulation.
3. **Act III (0:55–1:05)** — Cut to white. Quote card → constellation. Logo as relief.
4. **Act IV (1:05–2:38)** — The loop, told as Dana's story. Six beats.
5. **Act V (2:38–2:58)** — Three outcomes: churn → ICP → roadmap.
6. **Act VI (2:58–3:14)** — March quote, now findable. Upload a conversation.

## Variants

- **:30 paid** — Act I, Beat 1b, Beat 5, Act VI.
- **:15 social** — cold open, then the greyed-out question *"already answered — June."* No narration.
