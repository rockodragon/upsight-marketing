# CI-v2 — Killer Loop Homepage Hero

## Job
Make the product visceral in one glance: not a prettier survey — the machine that turns messy customer voice into decisions and proof.

## Visual thesis
**Left → Center → Right**

1. **Left:** moving feed of 3–5 customer snippets (calls / surveys / video responses)
2. **Center:** UpSight extracts one strong takeaway  
   e.g. *“Implementation confidence is blocking expansion.”*
3. **Right:** three outputs from the same evidence  
   - Product priority  
   - Internal stakeholder reel  
   - Customer story candidate

## Loop (spoken, optional VO later)
3 conversations uploaded → one cross-customer pattern → source evidence → decision brief → story reel

## Guardrails
- No generic dashboard cards as the hero
- No single talking-head as the whole story
- Brand: UpSight logo + name present by the hold beat
- Tokens from `30-strategy/brand-style-guide.md` only
