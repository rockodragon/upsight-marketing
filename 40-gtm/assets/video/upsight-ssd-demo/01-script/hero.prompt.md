# Prompt: SSD Demo — UpSight for Founders & Board Members

**Slug:** `upsight-ssd-demo`  
**Composition:** `SsdDemo`  
**Source:** `source-ssd-demo-script.md` (from `40-gtm/assets/scripts/ssd-demo-script.md`)

## Audience

Startup San Diego — board members interviewing founders; transferable to founders recording customer meetings.

## Format

- **1920×1080**, 30fps, **2:30** (150s)
- Screen recording + voiceover (record silent, narrate in post)
- Remotion version: animated mockups until captures land in `04-assets/`

## Scene structure

| # | Scene | Duration | Screen | Hook |
|---|-------|----------|--------|------|
| 1 | The Problem | 20s | Messy notes | Lost patterns after many calls |
| 2 | Record | 30s | Live transcript | Zero-touch recording |
| 3 | Interview Opens | 30s | Evidence panel | Receipts, not summaries |
| 4 | Themes Across Calls | 40s | Themes page | Cross-call synthesis |
| 5 | Person Profile | 20s | People profile | Every person tracked |
| 6 | The Closer | 10s | Clean UI | "UpSight gives you receipts." |

## Pre-recording checklist (for real captures → `02-capture/`)

- [ ] Project has 4–6 interviews already processed
- [ ] Themes page shows 3+ themes with 2+ evidence quotes each
- [ ] At least one person profile with clean name and history
- [ ] Desktop app tested with 2-min recording
- [ ] Pick best interview for evidence scene
