# Account Signal — consulting opener

**Not a survey VSL.** One wedge: the why behind at-risk (or lost) accounts — in the customer's words — with a receipt and a next move.

**Job (Colin / CS room):** When the scoreboard says fine, still know who's leaving — and what to do.  
**Flex (Richard / sales):** When a deal “should be fine,” know why it won’t — same spine.  
**Length:** ~92s · **Master:** 1920×1080 LinkedIn · CI-v2 paper · captions burned in

## Beats
1. **Opener (0–12)** — You have the scores. You don’t have the why.  
2. **Approaches (12–28)** — Another survey / CRM note / QBR — tidy, too late.  
3. **Lens (28–42)** — Words already live in surveys, calls, email → reason · receipt · move.  
4. **One screen (42–72)** — Fine 7/10 → follow-up → evidence → next action → ×13 *(illustrative)*.  
5. **Land (72–80)** — Receipt · Move · Pattern = Account Signal.  
6. **Ask (80–92)** — Two accounts you have suspicions about · 30 min.

## Meeting use
Lead with their blind spot, then this cut — or talk the spine live. Don’t demo product UI that isn’t demo-clean; keep illustrative stand-ins labeled.
