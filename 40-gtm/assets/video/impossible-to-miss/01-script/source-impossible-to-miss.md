# Impossible to Miss — creative treatment

**Job:** Brand film — all placements (homepage hero, LinkedIn, paid, board/investor).  
**Length:** 75s master (also cut 60s + 15–20s bumper + mute-safe)  
**Master:** 1920×1080 · 30fps · perception turn (cool miss → legible signal)  
**Tone:** Quiet confidence. Observational. No AI hype. No feature checklist voice.  
**VO:** AI TTS (ElevenLabs preferred; Deepgram OK) — calm prestige read, not announcer.  
**Music:** AI-generated, light — sparse → hold → soft clear bed.  
**Locked vignettes:** survey · churn · missed expansion · CS issues  
**v1 production:** Stock + photos + AI plates + Remotion. No live shoot. Illustrative UI. Illustrative receipts.

---

## The one idea

**Your customers are already telling you. UpSight makes those signals impossible to miss.**

Everything else (profiles, patterns, churn, expansion, reels) is proof of that idea — not the idea itself.

If a beat doesn't serve "already telling you → impossible to miss," cut it.

---

## Why this isn't "dark office → bright office"

Junior version: somber music, muted grade, product name lands, lights up, smile.

Chiat Day version: **the world doesn't change — your ability to see it does.**

Act 1 is *truth spoken and lost*. The turn is perceptual: overlays resolve into the same frames. Color and contrast return because the signal is now *legible*.

---

## The continuous ramp — what actually changes (the "wow")

Grade alone (cooler → warmer) is atmosphere. The **desire** comes from *agency appearing on the same picture*.

During the ~4–6s turn (VO: *UpSight turns every customer conversation into compounding signal*), run **one continuous plate** (or seamless morph of the same desk/call frame) and change content in this order:

### 1. Sound clears first (~0.5–1s)
Diegetic office noise ducks. Near-silence or a single held tone. Viewer leans in before anything pretty happens.

### 2. The lost thing reappears, labeled (~1–2s)
Whatever Act 1 just buried comes back *inside the frame* as signal — not a new location:

| We just watched… | On the ramp it becomes… | Desire unlocked |
|------------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| Survey tab closed | Free-text answer pins to a **living profile** | "Nothing falls through" |
| Churn call ends, laptop closes | **Risk** on that account before cancel | "I get time back" |
| Expansion insight walks away | **Opportunity** card + next move | "I see the money I was leaving" |
| CS third contact, no pattern | **Pattern** across tickets + recommended action | "Stop fighting the same fire" |

v1: pick **one hero turn plate** (strongest: survey→profile *or* churn→risk) and let the other three pay off in Act 3 reverses. Don't overlay all four at once — that reads as a feature dump.

### 3. Compounding chain (the product fantasy, ~2–3s)
On that same plate, show cause → effect in three beats max (illustrative, Remotion):

**conversation lands → profile updates → next move appears**

That's the "wow that's what I want": not brighter lights — **truth that used to vanish now accumulates and tells you what to do**.

Motion language: confident-glide ease; sky/amber only on signal layers; rest of plate stays naturalistic. No purple glow, no particle explosion, no logo sting yet.

### 4. Grade follows meaning (throughout the ramp)
Saturation/contrast lift *as* the overlays resolve — synced to the chain, not a separate "happy filter." Warmth = legibility, not cheerfulness.

### 5. What we refuse at the turn
- Hard cut to a sunny atrium / high-fiving team  
- Full product UI tour  
- Founder smile-to-camera  
- Big wordmark early  
- Checklist of features flying in  

**Test:** Mute the VO. If the ramp still reads as "I can finally act on what customers already said," it works. If it only reads as "the video got prettier," it fails.

## Act structure

### Act 1 — They're telling you (0–28s)
VO thesis. Picture: walk + four vignettes. Cool, underexposed, low sat. Light AI bed under diegetic. No type.

### Act 2 — The turn (28–48s)
Ramp as above, then short compounding proof (still illustrative). Sparse mono labels only: `// signal` · `profile` · `next move`.

### Act 3 — Proof (48–65s)
Four reverses + receipts card. Type: `Captured` · `Before cancel` · `Still time` · `Pattern` · `Receipts`.

### Act 4 — Land (65–75s)
Thesis close + wordmark. Air after last line. **No CTA in master.**

## CTAs (placement-only; paid bumper end card)

Master film never says "click here." Use these outside (or on paid end card only):

| Placement | Primary CTA | Alt |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| **Homepage** (under/ beside hero) | **See how signal compounds** | Get a walkthrough |
| **LinkedIn** (post / native) | **See UpSight** | Watch how signal compounds |
| **Paid bumper end card** | **See the signal** → getupsight.com | Book a demo |
| **Board / advisor rooms** | **30-min signal walkthrough** | Speak with an advisor |

Avoid: "Start free trial," "AI-powered platform," "Learn more" as the hero line. Prefer verbs that echo the idea (*see*, *signal*, *compounds*).

Paid end card (15–20s cut only): wordmark + one line ("Impossible to miss.") + amber CTA pill **See the signal**.

## Production — v1 vs later

| | **v1 (now)** | **Later** |
|--|--------------|-----------|
| Plates | Stock video, photos, AI environments | Live one-campus shoot, silent witness |
| UI | Illustrative Remotion | Demo-clean real screens where ready |
| Receipts | Illustrative quote/reel card | Cleared real customer clips |
| VO | ElevenLabs or Deepgram | Same or upgrade take |
| Music | AI-generated light bed | Custom/licensed polish |
| Overlays | Remotion 2D signal layers | Same craft, tighter plates |

Avoid uncanny AI "customer faces" as testimonials. Prefer hands, screens, backs-of-heads, silhouettes, real stock professionals graded to match.

## On-screen type (sparse)

- Act 1: none  
- Act 2: JetBrains Mono / amber operator labels only  
- Act 3: three words max per beat  
- Act 4: wordmark / closing line only  

Inter for any hero line; brand tokens only (`#050508`, sky, amber, `--ease-confident-glide`).

## What we will not do

- Feature laundry list as VO structure  
- Consumer-retail pain metaphors  
- Hard cut to "happy colorful office" as the reveal  
- Talking-head product pitch  
- Logo early; long UI tour  
- Uncanny AI customer faces as proof  
- Purple glow / generic SaaS motion
