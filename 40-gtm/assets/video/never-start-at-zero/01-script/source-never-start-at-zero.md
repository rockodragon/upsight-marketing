# Never Start at Zero — source VSL

> Ordinary forms start cold every single time. Yours never do.
> Every answer, every call, every reply *adds to a living profile* — so you always know who someone is, where they are, and what to say next. You're not collecting responses. You're compounding relationships.

Tone: warm, direct, human. ~2:15.

## Script

**[0:00 — Hook / pattern interrupt]**
*(On screen: a lonely spreadsheet row, cursor blinking.)*
"Here's the dirty secret about every form you've ever sent. The moment someone hits submit… their answer falls into a spreadsheet and *dies there.* No name you'll remember. No follow-up. No idea what to do next. And the next time you need something? You start over. From zero. Again."

**[0:18 — Name the real cost]**
"That's not a survey problem. That's a *relationship* problem. Because the people answering you aren't rows — they're customers at different stages. Some are curious. Some are ready to buy. Some just need one more nudge. And a flat list of responses can't tell you which is which."

**[0:36 — The shift]**
*(On screen: the spreadsheet row blooms into a rich person-profile card that keeps filling in.)*
"So what if it could? What if every answer someone gave you didn't just get recorded — it got *remembered?* What if each reply, each conversation, each call quietly built a profile of that person that got richer every time you talked?"

**[0:52 — The promise]**
"That's the difference. You ask a question once, and you never ask it again — because it's already part of who they are to you. Every interaction compounds. **You never start at zero.**"

**[1:04 — What you can actually do (the demo beats)]**
*(On screen: quick, satisfying UI moments matching each line.)*
"Now watch what that unlocks. You see, at a glance, *who's at what stage* — and who's ready to move. You follow up in one click, in context, because you remember exactly what they said last time. You turn a warm answer into a booked meeting without breaking the thread. And when you want to go deeper, you just… ask — over voice, over video, in the moment — and it feels like a conversation, not a chore."

**[1:28 — The feeling / simplicity]**
"And here's the part people don't believe until they try it: it's as easy to send as any form you've used. One link. Beautiful. One question at a time. Your customer feels *heard*, not processed. The power is all underneath — you just get to have a better conversation."

**[1:44 — Proof / the pitch story]**
"I'll tell you when it clicked for me. I ran one survey — one — and it didn't just give me answers. It gave me a *pitch.* Real quotes, real patterns, the exact language my customers used, stacked into profiles I could actually act on. I walked in and made the strongest case I've ever made — because for the first time, I wasn't starting from a blank page. I was starting from *everything I already knew about them.*"

**[2:04 — CTA]**
*(On screen: the compounding profile, glowing, next to a simple "Send your first ask" button.)*
"Stop collecting responses that die in a spreadsheet. Start building relationships that compound. Send your first ask free — and never start at zero again. getupsight.com."
