## What is customer call analysis software?

Customer call analysis software turns recorded conversations with customers and prospects into
structured, searchable evidence. Where an AI note-taker produces one summary per meeting, call
analysis software extracts the specific claims, objections, needs and decisions across many calls,
attributes each one to a speaker and a timestamp, and finds the patterns between them.

The category has two ends. At one end sit sales-coaching platforms priced for large revenue teams.
At the other sit note-takers that summarize a call once and file it. UpSight sits in between: built
for founders, product leads and consultants who talk to customers weekly and need the analysis, not
the coaching or the filing.

**In short:** a note-taker reads a call once. Call analysis software reads every call, together,
and shows its sources.

## Why does one summary lose most of what a call is worth?

You record the meeting. You get the AI summary. It goes to email and Slack and never lands in the
CRM. Two hundred calls in, the question your team is arguing about today was already answered out
loud in one of them, and nobody can find it.

The summary is the problem. One reader, one pass, one framing. The buyer's exact words about budget
are gone. The workaround they described in passing is gone. The moment they said what tipped the
decision is gone. What survives is a paragraph that sounds like every other call.

## How does UpSight analyze a call?

### Lenses: the same call, read four ways

A lens is an analytical framework applied to every conversation. Each one asks different questions
of the same recording:

| Lens | Reads the call for | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **BANT** | Budget, authority, need, timing | Sales |
| **Discovery** | Problems, workarounds, what breaks today | Product |
| **JTBD** | The job the customer hired you to do | Marketing |
| **Decision** | What was at stake and what tipped it | Leadership |

Those four are the ones on the homepage. SPICED, churn-risk and consulting/SOW lenses ship too, and
Team plans can define custom lens templates for their own workflow.

### Receipts: every claim links to its moment

Click any finding and the recording opens at the second it was said: video, audio or transcript.
If the answer isn't in the call, UpSight says so instead of filling the silence with a plausible
sentence. The same lens on the same call returns the same findings, so your team argues about the
evidence rather than the tool.

### Patterns: across people, not just within a call

Findings from every call cluster into themes with evidence counts. Each person keeps a profile that
grows with every conversation, so you can see what one customer needs and what many are telling you,
and click from the pattern back to the person and the source.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is customer call analysis software in simple terms?
Software that takes recorded customer and prospect calls and extracts the specific claims,
objections, needs and decisions in them, attributes each to a speaker and a timestamp, and finds
patterns across many calls. It replaces re-listening and one-paragraph summaries with searchable
evidence.

### Does it work on calls I recorded months ago?
Yes. That is the intended starting point. Upload three old recordings from any tool and UpSight
analyzes them the same way it would a call from this morning.

### How is this different from Fathom, Granola, Otter or a Zoom summary?
Those tools read a call once and produce one summary. UpSight reads every call through several
lenses, links every finding to its moment in the recording, clusters findings across calls into
themes, and keeps a profile per person that grows with each conversation.

### How is this different from Gong or Chorus?
Gong and Chorus are sales-coaching and revenue-intelligence platforms priced for large sales teams.
UpSight analyzes calls for the decision at hand, across sales, product, success and research, and
is priced for teams of one to ten. It does not do rep scorecards or call coaching.

### What file formats can I upload?
Audio: MP3, WAV, M4A. Video: MP4, MOV. Text: TXT, PDF, DOCX. You can also record live in the
desktop app. Transcription is unlimited on every plan.

### Can I trust the AI's findings?
Every finding links to the exact moment it was said, so you can check it in one click. If a
question isn't answered in the call, UpSight says so rather than guessing. The same lens on the same
call returns the same findings.

### Can Claude or ChatGPT query my analyzed calls?
Yes. UpSight ships an MCP server so Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP clients can search conversations,
read evidence and create tasks or opportunities from chat. Read-only on Free, read and write on Pro.
See the [MCP server page](/mcp-server).

### What does it cost?
Free forever for one project with five AI analyses a month and unlimited transcription. Pro is $29
a month for a single user with unlimited analysis. Team is $39 per user a month with a two-seat
minimum, and stakeholders view free. Details on the [pricing page](/pricing).
