# What is a customer intelligence platform?

**A customer intelligence platform is software that unifies customer conversations from every source — calls, surveys, interviews, notes — into a single evidence base that teams can search, analyze, and cite.**

The category consolidates work that used to require four tools: a meeting notetaker, a survey tool, a research repository, and a spreadsheet holding the synthesis. The consolidation matters less than the continuity. A platform that merely stores all four sources in one place has moved the fragmentation rather than solved it.

## Why it matters

Every tool boundary is a place where context is lost. When the call transcript lives in one system and the survey response in another, nobody can answer a simple question like "what has this customer told us over the past year?" without manual reconciliation. Teams pay for that gap in extra subscriptions, repeated questions to customers, and decisions made without the full record.

## Example

A founder evaluating platforms should ask one question of each: *show me everything one named customer has ever told us, across every channel, with the quotes.* Tools organized around projects or signals cannot answer it. Tools organized around people can.

## FAQ

**What should a customer intelligence platform do?**

Unify sources, keep a persistent record per person, surface patterns across people, and keep every pattern traceable to the evidence behind it.

**How is it different from a CRM?**

A CRM records the state of a commercial relationship — stage, value, next step. A customer intelligence platform records what the customer actually said and what it means. They answer different questions and are usually both present.

**Do small teams need one?**

A team running fewer than roughly a dozen customer conversations a quarter can manage in documents. Past that, reconciliation cost grows faster than the value of the notes.
