# What is conversational intelligence?

**Conversational intelligence is the analysis of recorded customer conversations — sales calls, discovery interviews, support calls — to extract what was said, what it means, and what should happen next.**

The first generation focused on sales coaching: talk ratios, keyword tracking, competitor mentions. The more durable use is research: treating every customer-facing call as a discovery interview that already happened, and mining the accumulated set for patterns nobody was looking for at the time.

## Why it matters

Most companies record hundreds of hours of customer conversation and analyze them one call at a time, for one purpose, then never look again. The unread archive usually contains the answer to the question the team is currently debating.

## Example

A team searching two years of sales call recordings for the phrase "spreadsheet" finds it in 60% of closed-won discovery calls and 20% of closed-lost — which tells them more about their ICP than the quarter's win-loss deck did.

## FAQ

**What does conversational intelligence software do?**

Records and transcribes conversations, then analyzes them for topics, questions, objections, sentiment, and follow-ups.

**Is conversational intelligence only for sales?**

No. The same recordings are research data for product, pricing, and positioning — often more valuable there than in coaching.

**Do you need consent to record?**

Recording consent requirements vary by jurisdiction and are frequently two-party. Confirm the rules where your participants are located before recording.
