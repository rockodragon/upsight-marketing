# What is a living customer profile?

**A living customer profile is a persistent, evidence-backed record of one person — what they've said, what they need, what's changed — that accumulates across every interaction instead of resetting with each new project, tool, or team.**

Most research tooling organizes around the study: a project, a round of interviews, a survey. When the study ends, the knowledge is archived with it. A living profile inverts that. The person is the durable object; studies are things that happen to them.

## Why it matters

Customers have one continuous history. Organizing knowledge around projects forces every new project to rediscover what an earlier one already learned, which is why customers get asked the same question twice. Person-level continuity is what makes the *next* question better, not just the next report.

## Example

Before a renewal call, the profile shows: three interviews over 14 months, a shift from "we need this to be faster" to "we need to explain this to our board," two unresolved requests, and the exact quote where they first mentioned the board. That is a different conversation than walking in with last quarter's NPS score.

## FAQ

**How is this different from a CRM contact record?**

A CRM record holds commercial state — title, stage, deal value. A living customer profile holds what the person has actually told you, with the quotes.

**Does it work for prospects, not just customers?**

Yes, and it is arguably more valuable there: what a prospect said during evaluation is what you need when they come back nine months later.

**What makes a profile 'living'?**

It updates from every new conversation automatically rather than requiring someone to remember to write the update.
