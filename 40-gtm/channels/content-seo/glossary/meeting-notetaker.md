# What is an AI meeting notetaker?

**An AI meeting notetaker is a tool that joins a call to record, transcribe, and summarize it, typically producing a summary and action items within minutes of the meeting ending.**

Notetakers solved the in-meeting problem convincingly: nobody needs to type while listening. What they generally do not solve is the across-meetings problem. Each summary is scoped to one call, and the accumulated archive of hundreds of summaries has no memory of the person across them.

## Why it matters

The value in customer conversations is mostly cross-sectional — what has this customer said over two years, what have 40 customers said about this topic. A per-meeting summary cannot answer either. Teams often discover this only after a year of diligent recording.

## Example

A team with 300 recorded calls can retrieve any single meeting in seconds and cannot answer "what has Acme told us about pricing?" without opening all of Acme's meetings by hand.

## FAQ

**What's the difference between a notetaker and conversational intelligence?**

A notetaker summarizes one meeting. Conversational intelligence analyzes patterns across many.

**Do AI notetakers replace research tools?**

No. They capture the raw material well and do not organize it around people or themes, which is where research value lives.

**Is it legal to record customer calls?**

Recording consent law varies by jurisdiction and is often two-party. Get explicit consent and confirm the rules where your participants are.
