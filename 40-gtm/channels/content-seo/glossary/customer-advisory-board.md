# What is a customer advisory board?

**A customer advisory board (CAB) is a standing group of customers who meet on a regular cadence to give input on direction, roadmap, and market changes — distinct from ad-hoc feedback because the same people return over time.**

The recurring membership is what makes it valuable. A board member's third session is informed by the two before it, which means the conversation can skip context and get to disagreement faster. It is also, in practice, a permanent solution to research recruiting.

## Why it matters

Most teams treat a CAB as a feedback ritual and under-use it. Its higher value is as a standing panel: people who have already consented to be contacted, already understand the product, and can answer a question within days rather than weeks.

## Example

A founder with a 12-person board can test a positioning change in 48 hours by emailing the board, instead of spending two weeks recruiting strangers who then need the context explained.

## In UpSight

Board members are person profiles with full conversation history, so preparing for a session starts from everything they have previously said rather than from last session's notes.

## FAQ

**How many customers should be on an advisory board?**

Small enough that everyone speaks — typically 8 to 15. Larger boards become presentations.

**How often should a CAB meet?**

Quarterly for full sessions, with the understanding that members can be contacted between sessions for quick input.

**What do you give board members in return?**

Early access, direct influence on the roadmap, and peer access to each other — which members often value most.
