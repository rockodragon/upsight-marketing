# What is win-loss analysis?

**Win-loss analysis is the practice of interviewing buyers after a deal closes — won or lost — to establish why they actually decided, as opposed to why the sales rep recorded that they decided.**

The gap between those two is the entire value of the exercise. CRM loss reasons are chosen from a dropdown by the person who lost, under time pressure, and skew overwhelmingly toward "price." Buyer interviews routinely find that price was the stated reason and something else — risk, timing, an internal champion problem — was the real one.

## Why it matters

Losses are the cheapest research available and the least used. The buyer has just spent weeks evaluating your category with full attention, and for a few weeks afterward they will tell you what they concluded. That window closes fast.

## Example

A CRM shows 60% of losses tagged "price." Twelve buyer interviews find that in eight of them the buyer never reached a price conversation — they dropped out during evaluation over an unanswered security question. The fix is a security page, not a discount.

## In UpSight

Win-loss interviews and call recordings land in the same evidence base as discovery research, so loss reasons can be traced to quotes and compared across deals.

## FAQ

**Who should run win-loss interviews?**

Not the rep who worked the deal. Buyers are markedly more candid with someone who has nothing to recover.

**How soon after the decision should you interview?**

Within two to four weeks. Detail decays quickly and the buyer moves on.

**Will buyers agree to a loss interview?**

More often than teams expect, particularly when the ask is framed as research rather than a second attempt at the sale.
