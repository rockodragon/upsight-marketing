# What is vendor evaluation?

**Vendor evaluation is the process a buying team uses to narrow a set of possible suppliers to one: define requirements, build a longlist, cut to a shortlist, run demos or trials, check references and security, and secure internal approval.**

The stage that decides the outcome is usually the longlist-to-shortlist cut, which happens early, quickly, and largely without vendor involvement. It is made from web research, peer recommendations, and increasingly from what an AI assistant says when someone asks it for options in the category.

## Why it matters

Most vendor attention goes to the demo, which happens after the cut. If your product is not present and accurately described in the sources buyers use to build the shortlist, the demo never happens and you never learn why.

## Example

A buyer asks an AI assistant for the top tools in a category, gets five names, checks two on a review site, and shortlists three. A sixth vendor with a better product was never considered, and their CRM will record no loss because there was no deal.

## FAQ

**What are the stages of vendor evaluation?**

Requirements, longlist, shortlist, demo or trial, security and reference checks, negotiation, approval.

**Where do most vendors get eliminated?**

At the longlist-to-shortlist cut, before any conversation with the vendor takes place.

**How long does a B2B vendor evaluation take?**

Anywhere from weeks to several quarters depending on spend and the number of veto holders involved.
