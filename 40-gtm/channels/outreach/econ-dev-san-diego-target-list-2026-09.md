## Read this before you use the list

**This file is a research map, not a CRM.** Per `CLAUDE.md`:

- **Sales pipeline lives in UpSight (MCP).** Do not track deals, stages, or next-steps here.
- **Contacts and enrichment live in UpSight / Apollo.** When a row here graduates to an actual
  target, create the person and organization records there. This file records *what is publicly
  knowable about the landscape*, so the next person does not repeat the desk research.
- **Customer evidence lives in UpSight.** Quotes from any of these orgs go there, not here.
- This file is *not* append-only. It should be revised in place as facts are verified or fall out
  of date, with the "accessed" date bumped on any row you re-check.

### The strategic order for this lane (do not reverse it)

1. **Grant reporting is the WEDGE.** It is painful, it is funded, it recurs on a calendar someone
   else sets, and missing it has consequences. That is what gets a first meeting.
2. **Early warning is the RETENTION story.** Spotting business distress or opportunity in a
   corridor before it shows up in lagging data (sales tax receipts, BLS, vacancy surveys) is why
   they renew. It is not the opening pitch.

Everything in the ranking below is scored against #1 first.

### Accuracy rules this file was built under

- **No invented people, titles, or email addresses.** Where a named contact could not be verified
  from a public source, the cell reads `not verified — find via LinkedIn/Apollo`.
- **No personal emails or phone numbers.** Org-level public contact info and named roles only.
- Every row carries a source URL and the date accessed.
- Anything that is the research agent's reasoning rather than a source's statement is tagged
  **[inference]**.
- Confidence is `high` / `medium` / `low`, with a note on what would raise it.

**Standing caveat on titles:** economic development staff turn over fast, and several of the names
below were confirmed from news items or third-party directories rather than a current staff page.
Re-verify any named contact on the org's own site immediately before outreach. Titles sourced from
ZoomInfo / RocketReach / TheOrg style aggregators are marked `low` on purpose — those are scraped
and frequently stale.

## 4. Business improvement districts, Main Street associations and corridor organizations

The City of San Diego has **18 City-designated BIDs**. Business owners inside the boundary are
assessed annually (typically $40–$500, newer districts $90–$1,200, anchor businesses up to $5,000);
the City collects via the business tax certificate bill and returns the funds to the managing
association **through annual contracts**. Associations must post meeting agendas, approved minutes,
bylaws, **audits, annual reports**, and contracts over $5,000 on their websites. That is a
recurring, contractually-mandated reporting obligation — adjacent to the grant-reporting wedge and
worth treating as the same pain.
