# The Decision Files — one-pager

**Use:** send to a practitioner before the site is ready. Ask them to poke holes.
**Owner:** Rick · **Date:** 2026-08-15

## What it is

Independent research on how security buyers actually decide.

One decision at a time: what the situation was, what was at stake, how the person
decided, and how it actually turned out. Primary interviews. Published.

## Why it doesn't exist yet

- **Analyst firms** ask buyers quantitatively — surveys, n=400, percentages.
- **Win/loss firms** ask qualitatively, but privately. The vendor pays; nobody else sees it.
- **Everyone else** covers vendors and products.

Nobody publishes rigorous qualitative research on how buyers actually decide.

> Everyone counts buyers. Nobody asks them properly, in public.

## Who's behind it

Rick Moy. Founded and ran an empirical product-effectiveness testing firm from 2007 —
independent testing sold into the Fortune 500, nearly acquired by Gartner. Same model
here: independence is the product.

## What a file contains

1. **30-second brief** — who, the decision, what was at stake, what they chose, what happened
2. **The situation** — in their words
3. **How they decided** — what they actually checked, and what they skipped
4. **What they'd do differently**
5. **Signals that mattered** / **Unknowns**
6. **Decision principle** — the portable rule
7. **Analyst counterpoint** — the strongest case against their decision

## The rules

- A real person, a real date, real stakes, direct quotes, and a real outcome — or it doesn't publish.
- **Named, role-only ("a CISO at a public fintech"), or anonymous.** Your call.
- You approve every quote before it runs.
- No vendor sees a file before publication. No sponsor touches subject selection, questions, or findings.
