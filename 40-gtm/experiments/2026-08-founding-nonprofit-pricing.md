# Founding-nonprofit pricing — $20/mo perpetual founding rate

- **Status:** running
- **Opened:** 2026-08-14 (revised same day: perpetual rate replaces 3-month step-up) | **Owner:** Rick
- **Checkpoints:** **2026-08-31** (redemptions; code expires) · **2026-11-01** (retention + usage cost)
- **Source question:** `00-control/open-questions.md` 2026-06-02 (packaging unit + entry number / WTP).

## Hypothesis
A capped, expiring founding rate — **$9 perpetual discount off Pro ($29) = $20/mo forever** —
converts the member-org referral wave now, and those founding accounts retain and generate
referrals + proof assets worth more than the $9/mo forgone. (Decision 2026-08-14: perpetual
founding rate chosen over the 3-month step-up bridge.)

## Mechanics
- Polar: $9 fixed-amount discount on Pro, duration **forever**, **max redemptions 5**,
  **expires 8/31**. `TAS-FOUNDING` (sent 8/14) + `FOUNDING-NONPROFIT` for the referral wave
  (Kateri + 4 inbound).
- The cap and expiry are load-bearing: a perpetual discount without a closed door isn't a
  founding rate, it's the price. After 8/31, member orgs pay $29.
- Each redemption trades for proof: case-study/testimonial permission + 2 warm intros at signup.

## Primary metric
Redemptions by 8/31; **retention of founding accounts** at 60/90 days.

## Guardrails
- Media/transcription cost per founding account — 7/19 memo flags low price + heavy media as the
  dangerous combo. Usage cost >$15/mo on a $20 account → cap usage or renegotiate.
- **WTP caveat (accepted trade-off):** perpetual pricing yields no step-up signal, so this
  experiment can no longer prove $29 is real willingness-to-pay. That question moves to the
  first **post-founding** member org asked to pay full price — track it explicitly.

## Decision rule
- ≥3 redemptions by 8/31 → member-org pull confirmed (feeds ICP head-to-head Lane B).
- Founding accounts retained at 90 days + ≥1 post-founding org pays $29 → pricing validated;
  close the 6/2 packaging question in `decisions.md`.
- Referral wave doesn't redeem even at $20 → price isn't the blocker; diagnose the offer.
