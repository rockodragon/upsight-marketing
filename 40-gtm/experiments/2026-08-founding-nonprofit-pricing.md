# Founding-nonprofit pricing — $20 bridge → $39 step-up

- **Status:** running
- **Opened:** 2026-08-14 | **Owner:** Rick | **Checkpoint: 2026-08-31** (redemptions) · **2026-12-01** (step-up survival)
- **Source question:** `00-control/open-questions.md` 2026-06-02 (packaging unit + entry number / WTP).

## Hypothesis
A time-boxed founding rate ($20/mo for 3 months via Polar discount, then auto-step to $39)
converts budget-constrained-but-engaged member orgs **and** survives the month-4 step-up —
proving $39 as real willingness-to-pay rather than charity pricing. The discount is a bridge,
not the price: list price stays $39 and every offer says so out loud.

## Mechanics
- Polar: `$19 off × 3 months` fixed-amount discount on the $39 Starter product; `TAS-FOUNDING`
  (1 redemption, sent 8/14) + `FOUNDING-NONPROFIT` (5 redemptions, expires 8/31) for referrals.
- Each redemption trades for proof: case-study/testimonial permission + 2 warm intros asked at signup.

## Primary metric
Redemptions by 8/31; **retention through the $39 step-up** (months 4–5).

## Guardrail
Media/transcription cost per founding account — the 7/19 memo flagged low price + heavy media as
the dangerous combo. If a $20 account's usage cost exceeds ~$15/mo, cap or renegotiate at month 2.

## Decision rule
- ≥3 redemptions by 8/31 → member-org pull confirmed (feeds ICP head-to-head Lane B).
- ≥2 of 3+ survive the step-up (Nov–Dec) → $39 validated as the floor; kill mercy-pricing forever.
- Most churn at step-up → $39 isn't real for this segment; the segment is proof/use-case, not ICP.
