# ICP head-to-head — consultants vs member orgs

- **Status:** running
- **Opened:** 2026-08-14 | **Owner:** Rick | **Decision date: 2026-09-08** (hard)
- **Source question:** `00-control/open-questions.md` 2026-06-01 ("which ICP do we lead with?") — open 10 weeks, never tested.

## Hypothesis
Member orgs (nonprofits, accelerators, associations) will convert to **paying** faster than
consultants/fractional operators, because pull already exists there (TAS paying + referring
unprompted — Kateri + 4 more inbound; SSD workspace active; media-heavy voice-of-community
work is where the product is differentiated), while consultants show polite interest but no
follow-through *when asked to pay*.

## The two lanes (both get real asks — that's the whole experiment)
- **Lane A — consultants/fractional ops:** the 6 recent conversations each get a written quote
  (~$1.5K/project or ~$200/mo per the 7/22 plan floor) + SDR outbound continues per comp program.
- **Lane B — member orgs:** `FOUNDING-NONPROFIT` Polar code ($20/mo × 3 months → $39, max 5
  redemptions, expires 8/31) worked through the TAS referral engine by Rick personally.

## Primary metric
**Paying customers per lane by 2026-09-08.**

## Guardrail
A lane only counts as *tested* if the asks actually went out (6 quotes sent in A; code offered to
≥5 orgs in B). "Cool conversation, no ask" = lane not run, not lane lost.

## Decision rule
- First lane to 3 paying — or more paying on 9/8 — becomes **primary ICP**; close the 6/1 open
  question in `decisions.md`; homepage/outbound/content all point at the winner.
- 0 paying in both lanes with all asks delivered → the problem is the offer or price, not the
  ICP; escalate to a pricing/packaging experiment, don't add a third lane.
- **No new lanes (event organizers, DTC, medspa, anything) until this resolves.**
