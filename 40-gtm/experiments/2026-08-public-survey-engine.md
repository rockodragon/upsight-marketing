# Public survey engine — product-as-marketing

- **Status:** planned (next up — starts when either running experiment resolves or by 8/22, whichever first)
- **Opened:** 2026-08-14 | **Owner:** Rick (runs survey) + Content role (posts) | **Decision date: 2026-09-19** (4 weekly cycles)
- **Source:** 30-day plan (7/22) Workstream 3 — written, never run. Uses the product as the demo.

## Hypothesis
One sharp public survey per week to the winning-lane audience (via UpSight, posted on LinkedIn)
will produce warm respondents who book calls at a higher rate than cold outbound — because
respondents self-select as having the problem, and the survey itself demonstrates the product
(the "I asked 20 X one question…" findings post is both content and proof).

## Mechanics (one cycle = one week)
1. Rick posts one question to the target audience, run through UpSight surveys.
2. Findings post ships (Content role, per rate card): question + what UpSight surfaced.
3. Every respondent gets a thank-you DM → soft invite to a 15-min chat (warm lead path).
4. Screen-capture of the tool doing the synthesis = demo asset for outbound.

## Primary metric
Respondents per survey; **respondent-DM → call booked rate** vs cold outbound baseline.

## Guardrail
One survey/week maximum — this feeds the content cadence, it doesn't replace the ask-for-money
motions. If a cycle ships no findings post, the cycle didn't happen.

## Decision rule
- DM→call ≥3× cold outbound rate after 4 cycles → survey engine becomes the standing top-of-funnel
  motion; budget content-role hours to it permanently.
- Respondents <10/survey for 2 straight cycles → distribution problem; fix audience before judging
  the mechanic.
