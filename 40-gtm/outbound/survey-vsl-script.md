# UpSight Survey VSL — "The Survey That Talks Back"

**Asset type:** feature-explicit VSL (sells the *surveys*, like the Hero). ~2–2.5 min, ~480 words.
**Merged best-of-two:** the churn cold-open hook + the "a form can't ask a follow-up" mechanism + the "10 shakiest accounts" CTA (from the survey-agent draft), plus the **video/audio media unlock** (from the first draft) — a second payoff the other version was missing.
**Two product levers it sells:** **Personalized Ask** (each contact gets their own questions, drafted from real history) + the **AI interviewer** (voice/chat, asks the real follow-up, captures it as evidence).
**Where it goes:** the `SurveyVSL.mp4` slot (rewrite), a surveys section on the site, LinkedIn (captions burned in), and outbound when the angle is feedback / response rates / testimonials.

Format: one beat per line. VO in quotes · `→` = what's on screen.

---

### COLD OPEN — THE HOOK
"This is the last thing a customer ever told us." → a single survey answer on screen: **"Fine. 7/10."**
"Fine. Seven out of ten. That was the whole answer." → the words sit there, alone.
"Three weeks later, they were gone." → a date stamp; "churned."
"The real reason was sitting in a conversation we never had. So why couldn't we see it?" → hold on the open question.

### THE PROBLEM
"Here's why." → 
"Every survey tool — Typeform, SurveyMonkey, Delighted — asks everyone the exact same questions." → identical forms fanning out to different faces.
"So people give you the polite minimum. 'Fine.' 'Seven.' 'No notes.'" → a wall of shallow answers.
"The answer that actually mattered never gets said —" → 
"— because a form can't ask the one question that would've pulled it out of them." → the form, frozen, unable to respond.

### THE MECHANISM
"A form can't ask a follow-up question. UpSight can." → land this line; it's the whole idea.
"Two things make that work." → 
"One — Personalized Ask." → label on screen.
"Every contact gets their own questions, drafted from your real history with them — the calls, the notes, the last survey. The survey already knows who it's talking to." → questions assembling from a profile.
"Two — the AI interviewer." → label on screen.
"When an answer opens a door, an AI interviewer walks through it — by voice or chat — and asks the real follow-up, the way a sharp researcher would." → a follow-up question appearing in response to an answer.
"And every answer comes back as evidence: the exact quote, who said it, when — not a blob of text in a spreadsheet." → quote + speaker + timestamp overlay.

### PROOF — TWO SCENARIOS (illustrative)
"Picture two of your accounts." → an "illustrative" tag stays visible.
"A quiet champion says the renewal 'should be fine.' The follow-up asks what 'should' means — and out comes the real blocker: their CFO just froze spend. Now you know, while you can still do something about it." → the answer expanding into the real reason.
"A customer mentions, almost in passing, that onboarding felt slow. UpSight tags it — and matches it to thirteen other accounts who said the same thing. One offhand comment becomes a pattern you can act on." → one quote linking to a cluster.

### THE MEDIA UNLOCK
"And because people can answer out loud — to camera, or just their voice —" → a respondent taps 'video' and starts talking.
"you don't only get the answer. You get it in their own words, on video." → the clip lands on a testimonial wall.
"The authentic testimonial you used to beg for, schedule, and chase — it just shows up in the responses." → beat.

### THE CTA
"So here's the ask." → 
"Bring your ten shakiest accounts to a thirty-minute walkthrough." → 
"We'll set up the questions that would actually get them talking — before they hand you a 'seven' on their way out the door." → callback to the opening answer.
"UpSight. Speak to an Advisor." → logo + CTA + cal link.
