# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is founder-led B2B sellers — solo founders and founding teams who still run discovery, demos, and follow-up themselves. Secondary audience is product and leadership teams who need customer evidence to decide what to build and ship next. Visitors arrive evaluating customer intelligence after living with fragmented tools (surveys, call notes, CRMs, research repos) that remember pieces of people, not the whole relationship.

## Product Purpose

UpSight is a customer intelligence platform that turns messy customer voice — calls, surveys, interviews, notes — into decisions and proof. Success for this marketing surface is not “understood the category.” Success is the visitor booking a **working session with their own conversations**.

## Positioning

Not a prettier survey — the machine that turns messy customer voice into decisions and proof.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: book a working session using the prospect’s own conversations
- Secondary CTA: Get UpSight / self-serve
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: **Not a prettier survey — a decision machine**
- Belief ladder:
  1. Category = customer intelligence
  2. Differentiation = person-level continuity across interactions
  3. The loop is visceral (voice → pattern → proof), not a dashboard
  4. Worth booking a working session
- Proof on hand: thin for now — no locked testimonials, case studies, or partner logos captured in this init. Treat named outcomes and retention evidence as open gaps until supplied under `.impeccable/assets/proof/` or vault collateral.

## Brand Personality

Sharp · Evidence-first · Urgent. Voice is direct and concrete. Prefer receipts over adjectives. The emotional job of the homepage and hero video is urgency — you’re leaking customer truth every week — without sliding into generic AI hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: purple glow, glassmorphism, tiny tracked eyebrows on every section, identical icon+heading+text card grids
- Pretty survey / feedback tools that read as chart dashboards
- Research repositories that feel archival and tag-heavy, not decision-making

## Design Principles

1. **Show the loop, don’t describe a dashboard.** The hero visual is the product argument: voice in → one pattern → decisions and proof out.
2. **Source variety beats tile uniformity.** Mix Zoom, calls, notes, and video so the feed reads as real work, not a stock marquee.
3. **Urgency with receipts.** Push action, but always ground claims in evidence language — never “AI-powered insights” as the reason to choose.
4. **Readable at video distance.** Large type, high contrast, generous spacing; decorative glow and chrome lose to clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable-first for now: strong contrast and large type on marketing pages and video frames. Formal WCAG targeting comes later. Prefer reduced-motion alternatives when shipping interactive web surfaces; captions for hero video are a follow-up, not a blocker for the current visual pass.
