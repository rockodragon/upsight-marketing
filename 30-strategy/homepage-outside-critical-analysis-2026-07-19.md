# Homepage Outside Critical Analysis

> **V3 correction:** V2 correctly identified person-level continuity as the strongest available
> product distinction, but overpromoted it into the homepage category and produced another dense,
> concept-first page. Dovetail now also describes customer intelligence as compounding in value.
> V3 therefore leads plainly with **customer intelligence platform**, uses **Know your customers.
> Not just their feedback.** as the point of view, and moves the non-resetting customer profile into
> the product proof. The strategic goal is category credibility first, differentiation second.

## Executive conclusion

The first customer-feedback homepage prototype was not differentiated enough to earn its CTA.
It described capabilities the market already expects from survey, research-repository, and
customer-intelligence products, then presented a single response as proof. The result looked like a
well-designed survey-analysis page instead of a new reason to choose UpSight.

The revised homepage should not lead with "analyze calls and surveys." It should lead with the
system-level advantage:

**Every customer conversation should build on the last.**

UpSight replaces the handoffs across five or more tools with a relationship-aware customer-learning
system. Each interaction enriches a living customer profile. That context improves the next
question, strengthens patterns across customers, and produces evidence-backed decisions and stories.

## Where the first prototype failed

### 1. It anchored UpSight in the least differentiated part of the product

The prototype emphasized uploads, survey responses, themes, source quotes, and testimonial moments.
Those are useful, but competitors already make these claims.

### 2. It described a pipeline, not a reason to switch

"Upload feedback and find themes" explains a task. It does not explain why a buyer should replace
their current combination of a notetaker, survey tool, research repository, spreadsheet or CRM, and
testimonial tool.

### 3. It showed one voice when the value comes from accumulation

The single-person video demonstrated capture. It did not demonstrate the higher-order value:
multiple interactions becoming a richer understanding of one person and multiple people becoming a
defensible pattern.

### 4. It asked for action before building enough desire

The visitor encountered an upload CTA before seeing:

- why the current toolchain loses context
- what a compounding customer profile changes
- how multiple conversations become a pattern
- how the same evidence serves product, leadership, marketing, and fundraising
- why UpSight can replace several subscriptions and manual handoffs

### 5. It confused an entry activity with the product's differentiator

Customer feedback analysis is a useful market-facing category. It should orient the visitor, but it
should not become the whole story. The differentiator is the integrated learning loop around a
persistent person and organization model.

## Competitive reality

### Dovetail

Dovetail already promises one platform for every customer signal, automated themes across calls,
support tickets and research, cited answers, customer clips, shareable documents, and organization-
wide action.

Implication: UpSight cannot differentiate with "multi-source insights," "customer intelligence with
receipts," or "share insights across the organization" alone.

Source: <https://dovetail.com/>

### Enterpret

Enterpret already connects feedback from 50+ sources, enriches it with user, account, opportunity,
revenue and product context, provides citations, and automates actions.

Implication: source breadth, business context, and evidence links are market parity at the
enterprise end. UpSight must show why its person-level context changes the next interaction, not
only how it improves analysis after the fact.

Sources:

- <https://www.enterpret.com/>
- <https://helpcenter.enterpret.com/en/articles/12665411-introduction-to-enterpret>

### Listen Labs

Listen Labs owns a clear speed-and-scale position around participant recruitment, AI-moderated
interviews, and comprehensive reports in hours rather than weeks.

Implication: "AI interviews at scale" is not an ownable homepage lead for UpSight.

Source: <https://listenlabs.ai/>

### Typeform

Typeform already supports text, audio, and video responses and transcribes recorded responses.

Implication: multimodal surveys are useful proof, but not sufficient differentiation.

Sources:

- <https://www.typeform.com/video>
- <https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/27965524569108-Collect-video-and-audio-answers-from-respondents>

### Fathom and AI notetakers

Fathom records calls, creates transcripts and summaries, supports search, and syncs summaries and
tasks into CRM systems.

Implication: recording, transcription, summaries, action items, and meeting search are table stakes.
UpSight must show what compounds across calls and why it knows the customer better over time.

Source: <https://fathom.video/alt>

### Senja and testimonial tools

Senja collects text and video testimonials, transcribes them, organizes them, analyzes sentiment,
and publishes them across web, social, and email.

Implication: "collect and share authentic testimonials" is also an established product category.
UpSight's stronger claim is that story-worthy moments emerge from the same ongoing customer
relationship used for research and decisions, without running a separate testimonial workflow.

Source: <https://senja.io/>

## Claims that are parity, not differentiation

Do not lead the homepage with any of these alone:

- analyze customer feedback with AI
- bring calls and surveys into one place
- find recurring themes automatically
- cite the original quote
- collect video and audio responses
- create customer testimonials
- share customer insights across teams
- turn feedback into action

They can support the story, but competitors can credibly say the same thing.

## Strongest differentiated combination

### 1. A living customer profile

UpSight organizes interactions around people and relationships, not anonymous responses or isolated
meetings. Goals, needs, challenges, preferences, behaviors, organizations, and evidence accumulate.

### 2. Compounding context

Each interaction adds to the profile. The system should know more after the fifth conversation than
after the first, without a human rebuilding context in a spreadsheet or CRM.

### 3. Context improves collection, not only analysis

The profile changes what UpSight asks next. This connects memory to adaptive surveys and AI
interviews, closing the loop between learning and the next interaction.

### 4. One evidence base serves multiple jobs

The same underlying evidence can support:

- product and service decisions
- customer follow-up and relationship continuity
- internal alignment
- authentic marketing stories and testimonials
- impact reports for sponsors and donors

### 5. Five or more tools collapse into one loop

UpSight can replace or reduce the handoffs among:

1. meeting recorder and notetaker
2. survey or interview tool
3. research repository and analysis tool
4. CRM, contact spreadsheet, or customer-memory system
5. testimonial and story-collection tool
6. manual reporting and internal synthesis documents

The economic argument is not only fewer subscriptions. It is less context loss, less manual
reconciliation, and fewer workflows asking the customer to repeat themselves.

## Revised homepage position

### Headline

**Every customer conversation should build on the last.**

### Subhead

UpSight remembers what each person tells you across calls, surveys, interviews, and notes. It builds
a living customer profile, asks smarter follow-ups, and turns patterns across conversations into
decisions and stories your whole organization can use.

### Consolidation proof

**One customer-learning system instead of a notetaker, survey tool, research repository, customer
spreadsheet, and testimonial app.**

### CTA

**Upload 3 conversations**

Support:

**See what they add up to.**

The number matters. A single upload demonstrates summarization. Three or more conversations begin to
demonstrate accumulation, patterns, and the product's differentiated value.

## Revised persuasion sequence

1. Promise that knowledge compounds across interactions.
2. Show multiple real customer voices becoming structured takeaways with the Remotion marquee.
3. Expose the fragmented five-tool status quo.
4. Show the living customer profile and how it becomes richer with each interaction.
5. Show how prior context produces a better next question.
6. Move from one person's profile to patterns across the customer base.
7. Show internal decisions and external stories coming from the same evidence.
8. Ask the visitor to upload three conversations or analyze them with the founder.

## Positioning caveat

"Living customer profile" is the strongest available territory, but it must be proven in the
product. Enterpret also connects feedback to users and accounts. The defensible claim is not merely
that UpSight stores person context. It is that person context compounds across interactions and
actively changes collection, follow-up, synthesis, and story creation in one workflow.

That loop should become the product demo, activation experience, and roadmap test. If the product
cannot yet demonstrate it end to end, the homepage should not overstate it.

## V4 conversion addendum

The differentiated mechanism above remains valid, but it should not carry the entire hero. The V4
homepage enters the established customer-intelligence category, names one champion, and turns the
strategy into a conversion trail:

1. outcome and category for lean B2B teams
2. three concrete reasons to believe
3. self-serve and founder-assisted analysis paths
4. visceral multi-conversation product proof
5. old fragmented workflow versus one continuous customer-intelligence layer
6. product mechanics, a three-step path to value, founder conviction, and a final ask

This follows Fletch's positioning discipline and the persuasion order demonstrated by the Standard
Issue template without copying its design or unsupported proof modules. Logo walls, integration
grids, migration-time claims, quantified outcomes, and customer testimonials stay out until UpSight
has evidence for them.

The approved V4 hero is:

**Customer intelligence for lean B2B teams**

**Know your customers. Not just their feedback.**

**UpSight brings calls, surveys, interviews, and notes together so product, leadership, and
marketing can see what customers need, why it matters, and who said it.**

Primary CTA: **Analyze 3 conversations**

Assisted CTA: **Analyze them with Rick**
