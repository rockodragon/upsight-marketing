# Wedge & ICP Refinement — 2026-06-26

*Purpose: sharpen the lead wedge and ICP based on UpSight's own customer-research data.*
*Provenance: derived from analyzing the "UpSight Core" research project (94 interviews, 1000+ evidence) inside the product, plus a hard-feedback strategy session. Refines — does not replace — [[positioning-brief]] and [[messaging]].*

> **TL;DR**
> 1. **Lead with the receipts/trust wedge.** It's value-prop #2 in the brief today, but it's the **#1 validated theme in our own customer data** (14+ independent sources). Make it the spear tip, not one of five.
> 2. **Add a psychographic ICP layer** on top of the demographic one: the buyer who pays is defined by a *belief* — *"I won't act on an AI summary I can't audit."*
> 3. **Distribution wedge: @claude-in-Slack** — query customer truth where the team already works. Timely, on-brand ("memory layer for distributed teams").

---

## 1. The wedge customers actually validated: receipts / trust

Our positioning brief lists five co-equal value props. Our **own customer research** says they are not co-equal. Ranked by evidence mentions across the UpSight Core project, the **#1 theme** is:

> **"AI Accuracy & Trust Issues"** — 56 mentions, **14+ independent sources**, marked *Validated*. Distrust of AI extraction, fear of false conclusions, the burden of auditing the AI's work.

Runner-up, same cluster: **"Balancing Human Oversight in AI Processes"** (52 mentions, 23 sources). The market's single loudest anxiety about AI tools is *"can I trust the output?"* — and our differentiator (**receipts: every insight auditable side-by-side to the source**) is the direct answer.

**Recommendation:** promote "Grounded, not generated" from value-prop #2 to **the lead**. Category framing:

> **AI customer intelligence you can defend** — receipts for every insight, auditable to the source.

Customer verbatims to use (all real, from our research):
- *"It's leading to false conclusions."* (re: AI research tools)
- *"The public AI that's available is just not very trustworthy."*
- *"Look side by side the source transcript and where the AI is pulling its themes."*
- *"How do we know it's pulling the right data?"*

This does **not** abandon the brief's other props — "engage the right people," "one place," "decisions not decks" still matter. It says: **trust is the door, the rest is the room.**

---

## 2. ICP: add the psychographic layer

The brief's ICP is **demographic** (startup founders 5–50, solo consultants, product/UX leads at SMBs, fractional execs). That's the right *who-by-headcount*. But it doesn't predict *who pays*. The data points to a **psychographic** cut that does:

> **The skeptic who's accountable for being right about people.**
> They run customer conversations, they have to *defend* decisions, and they refuse to act on an AI summary they can't audit.

- **Defining belief:** "I won't act on a black box."
- **Trigger:** the "says who?" moment — asked to defend a call about a customer and having only a vibe.
- **Already pay for** something adjacent (Dovetail, Gong, Otter) and have felt it produce "false conclusions."

**Anti-ICP (disqualify fast):** the *"just give me the summary, I trust it"* user. Low willingness to pay, doesn't value receipts. Our own data flagged this as a contrast persona to avoid.

**Why this matters for GTM:** the psychographic layer is the *message selector*. Same demographic, but the skeptic converts on "receipts / says who?", while the trusting user shrugs. Target the skeptic.

See also: [[icp/messaging-by-icp]], [[icp/README]].

## 3. Distribution wedge: @claude-in-Slack

UpSight already exposes its intelligence over an MCP server; Claude in Slack now speaks MCP. So:

> A teammate types **"@claude — what did Acme say about pricing?"** in Slack, and gets back **real quotes, attributed and timestamped**, pulled live from UpSight.

This is a 20-second demo that *shows* the receipts and meets distributed teams where they already work — directly on-brand with **"memory layer for distributed teams / chief-of-staff vibe."** Recommend it as a hero demo and a top-of-funnel content engine.

## Caveat: data hygiene (read before over-indexing on current auto-personas)

The product's current auto-generated personas/ICP for UpSight Core were **skewed by an attribution bug** — guest evidence was over-credited to the meeting host (the founder appears as the loudest "customer," and exists as ~5 duplicate person records). A fix + reprocess is underway. **Until then, treat the product's current auto-personas with caution**; the wedge and ICP above are drawn from the *theme-level* signal and verbatims, which are more robust to the bug than the per-person rollups.
